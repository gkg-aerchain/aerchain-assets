ACT 04

Autonomous sourcing — the live demo

Run on a live Adani-flavoured tenant, with a real sourcing event and vendor-side bidding.

GauravAerchain
Give me anything you'd want to buy — imagine you're a business user. From a vague request like "I want glass bottles," the system first checks whether an existing purchase framework can short-circuit the transaction — previously ordered items, hosted catalogs, framework agreements, punch-out sites — and pulls them into one comparative view. Then it asks category-aware clarifying questions: food-grade or pharma-grade, quantity, and so on. It summarises the requirement, auto-categorises it, estimates spend, sets a need-by date, and pre-fills a complete PR. The business user just submits — and the system executes the entire sourcing event end-to-end.
Adanitech team
Is this written on your own portal, or on Ariba? Basically, what is the platform here?
GauravAerchain
We're a one-to-one replacement for Ariba — but most companies can't do a big-bang S2P change, so we start with tactical and tail spend and integrate with Ariba. You have an intake workflow in Ariba; inside the Ariba workflow builder there's an API endpoint. When the workflow detects a tail-spend item, it pushes the Ariba PR to Aerchain; we execute the whole event autonomously and use the same API to update that original PR. The PR then completes its auto PR-to-PO conversion in Ariba and the loop closes. We do exactly this with Coupa, Zycus and Ariba today. Your guided buying and catalogs stay on Ariba; the high-volume, low-value tail spend routes to us.
Adanitech team
So onboarding stays ours, and the vendors here are only our own — not Reliance's vendors?
GauravAerchain
Correct — we're not a shared network. For every customer we work off your own vendor master; your vendors bid on our portal, fully ring-fenced. That said, our AI vendor-discovery can suggest additional vendors, and because IndiaMart is our core investor, our India vendor intelligence is genuinely strong — we're working to integrate the IndiaMart vendor network into our discovery module. But to be clear: it's customer-specific, closed networks, not a giant shared pool.

GauravAerchain

We've broken negotiation into a science — Round 1 is mutual discovery, Round 2 is benchmark pressure, Round 3 is the target push, and there's a "baffle" round. It builds a should-cost model, shows the benchmark source, sets the budget ceiling and parameter targets, and you can see its strategy and reasoning. It negotiates with as many vendors as you like, in parallel, and ends with a comparison and an award recommendation.
