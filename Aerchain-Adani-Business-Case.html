The Adani Group already runs one of India’s most digitally mature procurement functions — SAP Ariba and SAP Business Network across ~24 businesses, ~300,000 procurement cycles a year, of which ~100,000 become formal POs — the rest are catalog, non-PO, tail and invoice events — and ~94% first-time-right digital invoicing. The remaining opportunity is not digitisation; it is orchestration and judgment at scale, concentrated in the low-maturity tail, non-PO and tactical zone rather than the already-digitised strategic core. By layering Aerchain’s AI-native sourcing suite on top of the existing SAP estate, the Group can compress cycle times, redirect scarce buyer hours from administrative churn to high-leverage negotiation, and capture $0.45B–$0.75B/yr of conservatively-modelled, bottom-up category savings on its ~$12B of addressable & influenceable spend — while strengthening compliance, ESG screening, and audit governance.

The single message: Adani has built the rails; Aerchain runs the trains at the speed of thought — converting a mature-but-manual procurement engine into an autonomous one, with $0.45–0.75B of identified annual value (on ~$12B addressable spend) against a platform cost of ~$2.1M/yr.

2Cycle-time reduction (−40%, complex sourced categories)
Mechanism
Scoped to complex sourced categories (structured RFx / negotiated events). AI compresses the manual, sequential steps of a complex sourcing cycle. RFx documents are generated from templates and prior awards in hours instead of days; incoming offers are OCR-parsed and normalised to a common schema in minutes; each vendor is scored against technical and commercial criteria automatically; and approvals/NFAs are auto-routed rather than chased by email.
Resource & time
The saved days come out of buyer and category-manager hours and out of approver wait-time. A complex event that ties up a buyer for parts of ~24 elapsed days now clears in ~14 — freeing both the buyer’s active hours and the queue behind them. The ~24-day baseline is indicative, to be validated on Adani’s data.
