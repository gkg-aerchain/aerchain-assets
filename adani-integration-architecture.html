Context & Scope

What we're solving — and exactly where Aerchain sits

The immediate objective is to compress a slow, fragmented vendor-onboarding cycle and automate third-party due diligence, without disturbing Adani's investment in SAP and Ariba. Aerchain layers on top of the existing stack and integrates at three points — Ariba (front end), MDG (governance) and SAP (vendor master) — with a clear future-state option to go SAP-direct.

04
Vendor Onboarding · Step-by-Step

The onboarding flow, end to end

A single, owned journey from intake to an active, tagged vendor in SAP — with automated due diligence replacing the manual TBDT and email-based blacklisting. Each step shows the systems it touches.

1
Intake
Supplier self-registers on the Aerchain supplier portal, the buyer initiates on their behalf, or a vendor's Excel/PDF is uploaded and the AI extracts and pre-fills the form. Tax registration, PAN, bank and category details captured; form is fully configurable (down to a 10-question minimal set).
AerchainAriba SLP (initiation)
2
AI enrichment (Vendor 360)
The enrichment agent builds a full business overview from public and licensed sources — financial snapshot, news/media, industry ratings, category and web intelligence — so reviewers have context at their fingertips rather than chasing it manually.
Aerchain AI
3
Automated due diligence (TBDT replacement)
The due-diligence agent fans out parallel API calls — GST/MSME and PAN/KYC (India), OFAC and sanctions watchlists, DNB/Equifax financial and credit, adverse-media and legal/business risk — or plugs into your existing risk platform (e.g. ProcessUnity, as with Standard Chartered) and pulls the score in. Checklist is configurable from 30–40 possible checks; indicative cost < $0.50 per vendor.
ProcessUnity / DNB / OFAC / GST-MSMEAerchain
4
Risk scoring & human review
AI consolidates the checks into a risk view; a configurable human-review step precedes or follows it. Manual/physical verifications are captured through a recording UI so they enter the audit trail. Blocking/blacklisting becomes a systematic, auditable state — not an email.
AerchainHuman-in-the-loop
5
Approval (NFA)
Routed through your approval matrix; AI-generated summaries accompany each approval so approvers see exactly what is needed. Mobile approval supported.
Aerchain OrchestratorApprovers
6
Governed write-back to MDG → SAP
The approved record is pushed to MDG for governance/consolidation and on to the SAP vendor master (create/extend), or directly to SAP where MDG is not the gate — including the OEM-vs-dealer tag so downstream AI recommendations are policy-aligned.
AerchainMDG-S → SAP CREMAS/OData
7
Status sync to Ariba
Onboarding status and the active vendor are synced back to Ariba SLP so guided buying and sourcing continue to operate against a single, current supplier record.
Ariba Supplier APIs

Tail-Spend Autonomous Sourcing Loop

The second use case: close the loop with Ariba on tail spend

Beyond onboarding, the same integration pattern lets Adani route high-volume, low-value PRs to Aerchain for fully autonomous sourcing, then write the award back into Ariba — strategic spend stays exactly where it is today.

Closed Loop · Tail Spend
SAP / AribaPR raised; workflow flags a tail-spend item
Ariba WorkflowAPI endpoint pushes the PR to Aerchain
AerchainAutonomous RFQ → negotiation → award
Ariba PRAward & vendor written back to same PR
SAP / AribaAuto PR-to-PO conversion · loop closed
Proven pattern. This is how Aerchain runs alongside Coupa at Unilever (strategic spend on Coupa; tail spend autonomously on Aerchain) and is deployed across the Reliance Group on SAP + Ariba. Strategic, high-value spend continues through Ariba unchanged.

Phased Roadmap & Future State

Ariba-first today, with a clean path to SAP-direct

The answer to "Ariba or SAP — or both?" is: both, in sequence. We start on top of Ariba (lowest disruption), prove value on onboarding, then extend; if Adani ever retires Ariba, Aerchain operates directly against SAP as the onboarding and sourcing front end.

08
Master Data · OEM vs Dealer

Fixing the tag that lets AI recommend correctly

Adani's MDG today lacks a preferred-vendor / OEM-vs-dealer distinction, which blocks policy-aligned recommendations. Aerchain can consume an existing tag, or create and enrich it — and rationalize the broader master where data quality is an issue.

Tagging
OEM / dealer / preferred-vendor tags
Applied in MDG (or created by Aerchain where absent) so the AI suggests OEMs vs. channel partners correctly for brands like SKF, Schaeffler and NSK — and so fair, policy-aligned evaluation is possible.
Rationalization
Master-data de-duplication
A service-led capability to consolidate "many codes for one item." Native AI engine reaches 70–80% spend-matching accuracy out of the box, improving with human feedback.
Reference: for Tata Projects, Aerchain rationalized 1.5 million line items across their masters down to roughly 150,000 — the same approach applies to Adani's MDL clean-up.
