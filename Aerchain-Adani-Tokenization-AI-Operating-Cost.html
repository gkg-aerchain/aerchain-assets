How We Estimate This — and What’s in Scope

A fair question deserves a clear answer: “how do I understand the costing?” This is the honest, bottom-up answer — not a flat per-seat number, but the actual cost of the AI work each procurement cycle triggers. This is the internal fixed cost that sits underneath the Aerchain platform subscription.

∑
The model in one line
Cost = the AI work each agent performs, summed across every agent in a cycle, multiplied by how complex the cycle is, multiplied by how many cycles you run a year. Nothing else is in this number.
What’s counted
AI processing only
The direct cost of the AI engines (tokens) running every agent across the cycle, at current rates.
What’s NOT counted
Platform & infra are separate
Hosting, database, search, document storage, and the Aerchain platform subscription are not in this figure. This isolates the AI line so it can be reasoned about on its own.
Scope of the cycle
Intake → award → pay
The full autonomous spine: intake capture, RFx drafting, offer extraction, negotiation, evaluation, award — and the downstream invoice extraction & match, plus the always-on assistant agents.
⏱ A transparency note that holds for this whole document
These figures are based on current AI pricing. AI processing costs have trended down meaningfully year over year, but they can change in either direction, and the agent mix evolves as the product matures. Treat this as a directional planning model that we will re-baseline against live usage — not a fixed price.

A comprehensive map of the agents that run across a procurement cycle — 36 named agents, grouped by stage. Aerchain runs a combination of AI engines, routed per task across three tiers, so each job uses exactly the capability it needs and no more.

Deep
Frontier reasoning
Reserved for the highest-stakes work — per-vendor technical & commercial scoring, award scenarios, negotiation strategy. Used only where it changes the outcome.
Balanced
The everyday workhorse
Drafting, extraction and evaluation across most of the cycle — the best blend of quality and efficiency.
Fast
High-throughput
Lightweight engines for classification, ranking, routing and high-volume extraction — instant and economical.

Right-sizing the engine to the task — deep only where it matters — is a core reason the cost-per-cycle stays low.

A deliberate cost asymmetry — autonomy is highest where deals are simplest
Tail and standard deals run negotiation autonomously (the agent drafts and sends every round). Tactical and complex deals are buyer-led — the AI assists, a human drives. So negotiation cost actually peaks at standard and tapers on complex, where the spend shifts into deep evaluation instead. This is built into the per-cycle numbers.

The Four Complexity Tiers

Every cycle runs essentially the same agents — what changes is how much each one has to do: line items, vendors, questionnaires, documents, negotiation rounds. We band cycles into four tiers.

Tail
Simple, high-volume
1–3 lines, 2–3 vendors, no real negotiation, lowest-price award. Mostly MRO / operational / repeat buys. Runs fully autonomously on the fast tier.
Standard
Routine sourced
5–15 lines, 3–4 vendors, 1 negotiation round, light commercial evaluation. The autonomous-negotiation sweet spot.
Tactical
More moving parts
15–40 lines, 5–6 vendors, 2 rounds, commercial + light technical evaluation. AI assists; a buyer drives the negotiation.
Complex
Full techno-commercial
40–100+ lines, 10–12 vendors, full technical + commercial scoring, multiple award scenarios. Human-led negotiation; AI is the analysis engine.

What Actually Drives the Cost

◆
Complexity, not volume
The 20% of cycles that are tactical or complex drive ~48% of the bill. The 50% that are tail drive ~18%. Cost concentrates where the value is.
◆
Evaluation is the single biggest line
Per-vendor technical + commercial scoring on complex deals is the largest single cost — and the hardest work to do well.
◆
The mix is the master dial
Shift complex from 5% to 7% and the annual cost moves ~$36K. The single best way to tighten this estimate is to measure the real tier distribution.
◆
Costs trend down, levers exist
Batching the heavy evaluation work and routing simpler tasks to the fast tier keeps the cost efficient — before any provider price drops.

Running Aerchain’s full autonomous, intake-to-pay agent stack across ~300,000 cycles a year costs roughly $804K in AI at today’s pricing — about $2.68 per cycle.
