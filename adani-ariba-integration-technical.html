01
Context & Intent

The Problem We’re Solving

Adani is introducing Aerchain as an AI onboarding layer over an existing, working procurement estate. The goal is not to rip and replace — it is to make vendor onboarding and due diligence intelligent first, while keeping Ariba and SAP exactly where they are.

Two Horizons, One Layer
1
Near term — augment. Aerchain sits on top of the integrated Ariba + SAP stack as the AI onboarding and due-diligence brain. Ariba continues to run registration and sourcing events; SAP continues to hold the approved Vendor Master. Aerchain adds intelligence, governance and speed without forcing a cutover.
2
Eventual — absorb. As confidence builds, Aerchain progressively takes over the registration front-end and the sourcing capability (RFx and auctions), reducing dependence on Ariba on Adani’s timeline — never as a big-bang.
The question this document answers. A vendor cannot participate in an Ariba sourcing event without an Ariba Network ID (ANID). So if Aerchain becomes the onboarding brain, how does a vendor still get an ANID to bid — and how do the ANID, the SAP Vendor Master ID and Aerchain’s vendor record stay reconciled across registration, bidding, award and payment? That loop is what we close below.
02
As-Is

How Adani Runs Vendors Today

Three systems, one handoff point. The estate works — but onboarding intelligence and due diligence are manual, and vendor identity is split across two systems with no governed link between them.

  • SAP ERP — Vendor Master (system of record). Holds approved, transactable vendors. A SAP Vendor Master ID is created only when a vendor is formally onboarded — in practice, at award. Drives PO, invoicing, bank and tax.
  • SAP Ariba — registration & sourcing. Vendors register on Ariba and receive an ANID. With an ANID they can be invited to RFQs, RFPs and auctions. A large population of vendors are Ariba-only — registered and bidding, but never promoted to SAP because they never won.
  • Third-party due diligence — manual. Sanctions, adverse-media, financial and legal checks are run through a separate process, by hand, on email and spreadsheets — slow, inconsistent, and hard to audit or configure per business unit.

03
Reconciliation Model

Three IDs, One Golden Record

Aerchain does not introduce a competing identifier. It introduces the record that links the two that already exist — so a vendor can be tracked cleanly from first registration through to payment.

Bidding identity
Ariba Network ID (ANID)
Issued by Ariba at registration. The credential that lets a vendor be invited to and transact in sourcing events. Exists for every registered vendor, win or lose. AN01234567890
Transacting identity
SAP Vendor Master ID
Created in SAP at formal onboarding (award). The system-of-record identity for PO, invoice, bank and tax. Carries the heavy compliance burden. VEND-100xxxxx
Intelligence spine
Aerchain Vendor ID
The golden record. Opened at first touch, it links ANID ↔ SAP ID, carries the staged due-diligence state, risk score and document vault, and is the one place a vendor’s full lifecycle is reconciled. AER-VND-xxxxx
The linking principle. The Aerchain Vendor ID is keyed to the ANID the moment a vendor registers, and bound to the SAP Vendor Master ID the moment one is created at award. Whichever system a query starts from, the other two identities resolve through the golden record.
04
To-Be Operating Model

Staged Onboarding, Dual Entry

Both registration channels stay open — vendors can start in Aerchain or in Ariba — with Aerchain as the primary, governing record either way. Due diligence is staged: light to qualify for bidding, deep to qualify for the SAP master at award.

0
Stage 0 · Registration — dual entry, Aerchain-primary
Capture the vendor and secure an ANID
Aerchain-led path: the vendor registers through Aerchain’s AI-assisted portal; Aerchain validates and provisions the ANID from Ariba in the background. Ariba-led path (legacy / buyer-initiated): the vendor registers on Ariba’s existing front-end, and the registration payload plus the new ANID are pushed to Aerchain. Either way, Aerchain opens the golden vendor record keyed on the ANID — so there is a single, reconciled record from the first touch.
AI doc extractionANID provisioned / receivedGolden record opened
1
Stage 1 · Pre-approval to bid — light AI due diligence
Qualify for sourcing without touching SAP
Aerchain runs configurable, light-touch AI due diligence — sanctions, adverse-media and basic KYC to whatever depth Adani sets for “allowed to bid.” On pass, Aerchain writes a “Pre-approved for sourcing” status back to Ariba against the ANID, and the vendor can participate in events. No SAP Vendor Master is created yet — keeping SAP clean of the large non-winning bidder population, exactly as today’s gate intends.
Per-BU risk configWrite-back: pre-approved → AribaSAP untouched
2
Stage 2 · Full onboarding at award — deep due diligence
Promote the winner cleanly into the SAP master
When a vendor wins an event, Aerchain triggers full onboarding due diligence — financial, legal, banking, tax and document validation. On pass, Aerchain triggers SAP Vendor Master creation, the SAP Vendor Master ID is generated, and Aerchain binds ANID ↔ SAP ID in the golden record and syncs the linkage back to Ariba. The vendor is now transactable end-to-end.

3
Stage 3 · Steady state — continuous intelligence
Keep all three identities reconciled, for life
Aerchain becomes the intelligence spine: ongoing risk monitoring, re-certification, vendor 360 and performance scoring, with block / unblock decisions propagated to both Ariba and SAP. Ariba stays the event engine; SAP stays the master and finance system; Aerchain keeps them in sync.
Re-certificationVendor 360Block propagation
The loop, closed. The ANID is still minted by Ariba at registration — so the vendor can always transact on Ariba. Aerchain governs who is allowed to bid through light diligence, and defers the heavy SAP Vendor Master creation to award. The golden record reconciles ANID, SAP ID and Aerchain’s intelligence across the whole lifecycle. No vendor is ever stranded without a way to bid, and SAP is never polluted with vendors who never won.

Integration methods

  • API / event (preferred). Real-time registration sync, ANID exchange, status write-back to Ariba, and master-creation trigger to SAP via standard interfaces (OData / IDoc / BAPI on the SAP side, Ariba APIs on the network side).
  • File-based (fallback). Where a real-time interface isn’t available, scheduled secure file exchange keeps the golden record, Ariba status and SAP master aligned — same model, lower coupling.

Edge Cases

Closing the Loop

The scenarios that usually break a layered model — and how the staged, dual-entry design handles each without stranding a vendor or polluting the master.

ScenarioHow it’s handled
Vendor needs to bid but has no ANIDAriba mints the ANID at registration in both entry paths — Aerchain-led (provisioned in the background) or Ariba-led (received). The vendor always has a bidding identity before an event.
Vendor registers twice (Ariba + Aerchain)Aerchain de-duplicates on tax ID / ANID and merges into one golden record, so dual entry never creates two vendors.
Ariba-only vendor, never promoted to SAPStays bid-eligible on its ANID with a light-diligence record in Aerchain. No SAP master is created until — and unless — it wins.
Award → promotion to SAPDeep diligence runs, SAP Vendor Master ID is generated, and Aerchain binds ANID ↔ SAP ID and syncs the link back to Ariba.
Vendor blocked / blacklistedDecision is made in Aerchain and propagated to Ariba (network status) and SAP (posting block) so the vendor can neither bid nor transact.
Re-registration / re-certificationDiligence state is re-evaluated against the existing golden record — no new identity, full history retained and audit-ready.
08
Sequencing

A Path, Not a Cutover

Value lands in the first phase; Ariba dependence reduces only as Adani chooses. Nothing here requires switching off a working system on day one.

Phase 1 · Augment
AI onboarding & due diligence over Ariba + SAP
Golden record, dual-entry registration, staged AI diligence, “pre-approved for sourcing” write-back, and SAP master-creation trigger at award. Ariba and SAP unchanged.
Phase 2 · Orchestrate
Deeper reconciliation & automation
Full ANID↔SAP sync, automated master maintenance, vendor 360, re-certification, performance scoring and block propagation across both systems.
Phase 3 · Absorb
Eventual sourcing on Aerchain
Aerchain progressively takes on the registration front-end and sourcing (RFx / auctions), reducing Ariba dependence on Adani’s timeline — not as a big-bang.
Guiding principle — augment first, replace later. Aerchain earns the right to take over sourcing by proving the onboarding and due-diligence layer first. Adani keeps optionality at every step.
