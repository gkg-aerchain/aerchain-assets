01
Scope

One integration question, answered three ways

This document is deliberately narrow: it covers the vendor-onboarding integration only. Aerchain inserts as the AI onboarding and due-diligence layer on top of the systems Adani already runs, integrating at three points — Ariba as the front end, MDG for governance, and SAP as the vendor master — with no rip-and-replace.

Front end
SAP Ariba
Where users initiate and see onboarding today. Aerchain plugs in via Ariba's Supplier APIs and keeps SLP in sync.
Governance
SAP MDG
Master-data governance — deduplication, consolidation and vendor classification. Aerchain writes clean, governed records through MDG-S.
System of record
SAP S/4HANA
The vendor master. Aerchain creates/extends records and reads GRN data back for performance — across 14+ instances.
The three questions this answers — •  Ariba, SAP, or both initially? Both, in sequence — Ariba as the front end, SAP for vendor-master write-back from day one (§3). •  How does onboarding intersect Ariba via API, and tie to the SAP vendor master? Ariba Supplier APIs + webhooks for the front end; MDG-S / IDoc CREMAS / OData to SAP (§4–5). •  How does it fix slow, fragmented onboarding? One owned, SLA-tracked workflow replaces the multi-team, email-driven hand-offs — with duplicate-vendor checks before anything reaches the master (§2–4).
02
Current State · As-Is

Today, onboarding is split across systems, teams and inboxes — slow, manual, hard to govern

Ariba is the front end, MDG governs the master, SAP holds the vendor record. But the onboarding journey itself is owned by no single system: steps are handed between teams over email, due diligence and blacklisting run outside the platform, and the same vendor often lands in the master under multiple codes.

Onboarding Integration Flow

From intake to an active, governed vendor in SAP — one owned journey

One system owns the vendor end-to-end, with status and SLA visible at every step. Automated due diligence replaces the manual TBDT and the email-based blacklist.

Step 1Intake
Supplier self-registers, the buyer initiates on their behalf, or a vendor Excel/PDF is uploaded and the AI extracts and pre-fills the form — in English, Hindi or Gujarati. Tax registration, PAN, bank and category details are captured and validated at source, and the record is matched against the existing vendor master to catch duplicates before they ever reach MDG. The form is configurable down to a minimal 10-question set.
AerchainAriba SLP (initiation)
Step 2AI enrichment (Vendor 360)
The enrichment agent builds a full business overview from public and licensed sources — financial snapshot, news/media, ratings, category and web intelligence — so reviewers have context at their fingertips instead of chasing it.
Aerchain AI
Step 3Automated due diligence (TBDT replacement)
The due-diligence agent fans out parallel checks — GST/MSME & PAN/KYC, OFAC and sanctions, DNB/Equifax financial, adverse-media and legal risk — or plugs into an existing risk platform (e.g. ProcessUnity) and pulls the score in. Checklist configurable from 30–40 possible checks.
DNB / OFAC / GST · MSME / PANAerchain DD agent
Step 4Risk scoring & human review
AI consolidates the checks into a risk view; a configurable human-review step precedes or follows it. Manual/physical verifications are captured through a recording UI so they enter the audit trail — blocking/blacklisting becomes a systematic, auditable state rather than an email.
AerchainHuman-in-the-loop
Step 5Approval (NFA)
Routed through your approval matrix, with AI-generated summaries so approvers see exactly what is needed. Mobile approval supported.
Aerchain OrchestratorApprovers
Step 6Governed write-back to MDG → SAP — clean, classified, de-duplicated
The approved record is pushed to MDG for governance and consolidation, then on to the SAP vendor master (create/extend) — de-duplicated and carrying its full classification (preferred status, OEM / distributor / dealer, category, risk tier) so downstream sourcing and AI recommendations stay policy-aligned. The precise governance path through MDG is confirmed in the integration deep-dive.
AerchainMDG-S → SAP CREMAS / OData
Step 7Status sync to Ariba
Onboarding status and the active vendor sync back to Ariba SLP so guided buying and sourcing continue against a single, current supplier record.
Ariba Supplier APIs
Step 8Ongoing lifecycle & re-verification
Onboarding isn't a one-time event. Scheduled re-verification, document- and certificate-expiry alerts, and tier-, compliance- or performance-triggered re-onboarding keep the master clean over time — with GRN-based performance feeding back so the vendor record stays current without manual chasing.
AerchainSAP GRN feedback

Beyond Onboarding · Extensibility Preview

The same integration pattern extends — to tail-spend autonomous sourcing

Beyond the onboarding scope of this document: the identical, configured-not-coded pattern lets Ariba route tail-spend PRs to Aerchain for autonomous sourcing and write the award back to the same PR. Your guided buying, catalogs and strategic spend stay on Ariba, unchanged.

Tail-Spend Loop · extensibility
SAP / AribaPR raised; workflow flags tail spend
Ariba WorkflowAPI endpoint pushes the PR
AerchainAutonomous RFQ → negotiation → award
Ariba PRAward written back to same PR
SAP / AribaAuto PR-to-PO · loop closed
Proven pattern. This is how Aerchain runs alongside Coupa at Unilever — strategic spend stays on the incumbent suite, tail spend executes autonomously on Aerchain — and how it is deployed across the Reliance Group on SAP. In every case the incumbent stays in place: at Adani, strategic, high-value spend continues through Ariba unchanged.
