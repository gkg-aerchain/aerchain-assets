01
Scope

One integration question, answered three ways

This document is deliberately narrow: it covers the vendor-onboarding integration only. Aerchain inserts as the AI onboarding and due-diligence layer on top of the systems Adani already runs, integrating at three points — Ariba as the front end, MDG for governance, and SAP as the vendor master — with no rip-and-replace.

Front end
SAP Ariba
Where users initiate and see onboarding today. Aerchain plugs in via Ariba's Supplier APIs and keeps SLP in sync.
Governance
SAP MDG
Master-data governance and the home of the new OEM-vs-dealer tag. Aerchain writes governed records through MDG-S.
System of record
SAP S/4HANA
The vendor master. Aerchain creates/extends records and reads GRN data back for performance — across 14+ instances.
The three questions this answers — •  Ariba, SAP, or both initially? Both, in sequence — Ariba as the front end, SAP for vendor-master write-back from day one (§3). •  How does onboarding intersect Ariba via API, and tie to the SAP vendor master? Ariba Supplier APIs + webhooks for the front end; MDG-S / IDoc CREMAS / OData to SAP (§4–5). •  Future state if Ariba is replaced? Aerchain integrates SAP-direct, no Ariba intermediary (§6).

04
Onboarding Integration Flow

From intake to an active, tagged vendor in SAP — one owned journey

Each step shows the systems it touches. Automated due diligence replaces the manual TBDT and the email-based blacklist.

Step 1Intake
Supplier self-registers, the buyer initiates on their behalf, or a vendor Excel/PDF is uploaded and the AI extracts and pre-fills the form. Tax registration, PAN, bank and category details captured; the form is configurable down to a minimal 10-question set.
AerchainAriba SLP (initiation)
Step 2AI enrichment (Vendor 360)
The enrichment agent builds a full business overview from public and licensed sources — financial snapshot, news/media, ratings, category and web intelligence — so reviewers have context at their fingertips instead of chasing it.
Aerchain AI
Step 3Automated due diligence (TBDT replacement)
The due-diligence agent fans out parallel checks — GST/MSME & PAN/KYC, OFAC and sanctions, DNB/Equifax financial, adverse-media and legal risk — or plugs into an existing risk platform (e.g. ProcessUnity) and pulls the score in. Checklist configurable from 30–40 possible checks.
ProcessUnity / DNB / OFAC / GST-MSMEAerchain
Step 4Risk scoring & human review
AI consolidates the checks into a risk view; a configurable human-review step precedes or follows it. Manual/physical verifications are captured through a recording UI so they enter the audit trail — blocking/blacklisting becomes a systematic, auditable state rather than an email.
AerchainHuman-in-the-loop
Step 5Approval (NFA)
Routed through your approval matrix, with AI-generated summaries so approvers see exactly what is needed. Mobile approval supported.
Aerchain OrchestratorApprovers
Step 6Governed write-back to MDG → SAP, with the OEM/dealer tag
The approved record is pushed to MDG for governance/consolidation and on to the SAP vendor master (create/extend) — including the OEM-vs-dealer tag (SKF, Schaeffler, NSK and their channel partners) so downstream AI recommendations are policy-aligned. Where MDG is not the gate, Aerchain writes to SAP directly.
AerchainMDG-S → SAP CREMAS / OData
Step 7Status sync to Ariba
Onboarding status and the active vendor sync back to Ariba SLP so guided buying and sourcing continue against a single, current supplier record.
Ariba Supplier APIs

Beyond Onboarding

The same pattern closes the loop on tail spend — and scales to SAP-direct

The identical integration approach lets Ariba route tail-spend PRs to Aerchain for autonomous sourcing and write the award back. And if Ariba is ever retired, the same connectors point straight at SAP.
